> Where to launch and list your AI app: launch platforms, AI directories, app stores, review sites, newsletters and communities.

You built the thing. Now it needs to be in front of people. This list collects the places that accept AI products and says what each one costs.

**Rules of this list:** every site was opened and checked on the verified date: the site is live, submissions are open, and the free-vs-paid column reflects what the site itself says. Each line links to the real submission page, not just the homepage. Details of how entries are checked are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Part of a series of use-case directories. Last full check: 2026-10-18.

## Launch platforms

Sites built around launch day: post your product, get votes and feedback. Best used one or two at a time, with a real launch post, not all at once.

- [BetaList](https://betalist.com) - Features pre-launch and recently launched startups. All submissions are paid now, with an automatic full refund if your startup is not selected. Your product needs its own domain. **Cost:** Paid (full refund if not selected). [Submit here](https://betalist.com/submit).
- [Fazier](https://fazier.com) - Daily product launch platform. Free launch tier, plus paid options ($29-$69) with extra visibility. **Cost:** Free + paid from $29. [Submit here](https://fazier.com/submit).
- [Hacker News (Show HN)](https://news.ycombinator.com) - Post a Show HN when your product is ready to try. Huge technical audience with strict rules: read the Show HN guidelines (news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) before posting. **Cost:** Free. [Submit here](https://news.ycombinator.com/submit).
- [LaunchIgniter](https://launchigniter.com) - Launch scheduling platform. Sign in with Google or GitHub and schedule your launch day. **Cost:** Free. [Submit here](https://launchigniter.com/submit).
- [Microlaunch](https://microlaunch.net) - Launch platform with a monthly competition. Free launch; paid tiers skip the queue ($39) or add featured spots and boosts ($129). **Cost:** Free + paid from $39. [Submit here](https://microlaunch.net/submit).
- [Peerlist Launchpad](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) - Weekly product launches inside the Peerlist professional network. Free with a Peerlist account. **Cost:** Free. [Submit here](https://peerlist.io/launchpad).
- [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com) - The biggest launch stage for new tech products. Launching is free after sign-up; promoted 'Boosted' placements are paid ads. **Cost:** Free (paid boosts optional). [Submit here](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/new).
- [TinyLaunch](https://www.tinylaunch.com) - Small launch platform. Standard launch is free and sits on the homepage for days; Premium ($39 per launch) gets showcased between top spots. **Cost:** Free + $39 premium. [Submit here](https://www.tinylaunch.com/submit).
- [Uneed](https://www.uneed.best) - Daily product launches with community voting. Free launch queue; paid options skip the line ($29.99), feature the listing, or add a premium yearly plan. **Cost:** Free + paid boosts from $14.99. [Submit here](https://www.uneed.best/submit-a-tool).

## AI tool directories

Catalogs of AI tools that send long-tail traffic and a backlink. Many charge a review or listing fee; the cost column says which.

- [Dang.ai](https://dang.ai) - AI tools directory. The free listing needs a crawlable backlink and takes up to 4 weeks; $49 skips the backlink and adds a Featured badge; Pro is $30 setup plus $19/mo. **Cost:** Free with backlink, or $49. [Submit here](https://dang.ai/submit).
- [Easy With AI](https://easywithai.com) - AI tools directory with permanent dofollow listings. $125 one-time fee; free for free or open-source education and research tools, 50% off for paid ones in those categories. **Cost:** $125 one-time (free for education/research tools). [Submit here](https://easywithai.com/submit-tool/).
- [Future Tools](https://www.futuretools.io) - Matt Wolfe's curated AI tools directory. Free submission, manually reviewed before listing. **Cost:** Free. [Submit here](https://www.futuretools.io/submit-a-tool).
- [Futurepedia](https://www.futurepedia.io) - Big AI tools directory for work tools, claiming 400k+ monthly professionals. Paid listings only ($247-$497 one-time). **Cost:** Paid from $247. [Submit here](https://www.futurepedia.io/submit-tool).
- [SaaSHub](https://www.saashub.com) - Software alternatives directory. Its /submit page doubles as a free, actively monitored list of 110 sites where you can submit your product. **Cost:** Free. [Submit here](https://www.saashub.com/submit).
- [The GEO Directory](https://thegeodirectory.com) - Curated catalog of AI search (GEO) tools, where practitioners choose tools and vendors get found. Submissions are reviewed by hand; a paid Featured badge adds visibility without changing ranking. **Cost:** Free + paid featured. [Submit here](https://thegeodirectory.com/submit).
- [The Rundown AI (tool submission)](https://www.therundown.ai) - AI newsletter with 1M+ readers. Its tool directory takes free submissions; newsletter ads are paid separately (see Newsletters section). **Cost:** Free. [Submit here](https://www.therundown.ai/submit).
- [There's An AI For That](https://theresanaiforthat.com) - The largest AI tools directory, with a 2.5M-subscriber newsletter. Basic listing is a $49 one-time review fee, $347 with priority review. Once a month they run an X thread where one indie tool gets listed free. **Cost:** Paid from $49 (monthly free route). [Submit here](https://theresanaiforthat.com/submit/).
- [Toolify](https://www.toolify.ai) - High-traffic AI tools directory. Free submission form for tools and GPTs. **Cost:** Free. [Submit here](https://www.toolify.ai/submit).
- [Toolpilot](https://www.toolpilot.ai) - AI tools directory. Free listing requires adding their badge or logo to your site first (link back). **Cost:** Free with link back. [Submit here](https://www.toolpilot.ai/pages/submit-your-ai-tool).
- [TopAI.tools](https://topai.tools) - AI tools directory with editorial review. $47 one-time listing, $229 featured for 7 days. Automatic refund if your tool is not accepted. **Cost:** Paid from $47. [Submit here](https://topai.tools/submit).
- [Vantaige](https://vantaige.io) - AI tools directory and media hub: 600+ curated tools, a prompts library and AI calculators. Free basic listing needs a backlink and a 2-4 week review queue. Paid: Verified badge $25 one-time, Featured $49/30 days or $99/90 days, Spotlight $189 for 90 days with homepage placement and a sponsored story. **Cost:** Free (backlink required; paid from $25). [Submit here](https://vantaige.io/list-your-ai-tool).

## App, plugin and agent stores

Marketplaces where your product lives inside another platform: ChatGPT, Chrome, WordPress, Hugging Face, MCP registries and deal marketplaces.

- [AppSumo](https://www.appsumo.com) - Marketplace known for lifetime-deal software launches. Applying as a partner is free; AppSumo takes a revenue share on sales. **Cost:** Free to apply (revenue share). [Submit here](https://sell.appsumo.com).
- [Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore) - Home for Chrome extensions. One-time $5 developer registration, then publishing is free after review. **Cost:** $5 one-time. [Submit here](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/register).
- [Glama](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers) - Open MCP registry with 100k+ servers. Add your server with a glama.json file and claim the listing to manage it. **Cost:** Free. [Submit here](https://glama.ai/mcp/faq).
- [GPT Store (ChatGPT)](https://chatgpt.com/gpts) - OpenAI's store for custom GPTs, inside ChatGPT. Publish from the GPT builder; eligibility depends on your plan. **Cost:** Free. [Submit here](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8798878-building-and-publishing-a-gpt).
- [Hugging Face Spaces](https://huggingface.co/spaces) - Host a live demo of your AI app. Free CPU tier; the AI community browses trending Spaces. **Cost:** Free. [Submit here](https://huggingface.co/new-space).
- [mcp.so](https://mcp.so) - Large MCP server directory. Free submission with review; $39 one-time publishes immediately with a verified badge and a dofollow link. **Cost:** Free with review, or $39. [Submit here](https://mcp.so/submit).
- [Smithery](https://smithery.ai) - MCP server registry. Publish once, install anywhere, with built-in auth and observability. Sign in to publish. **Cost:** Free. [Submit here](https://smithery.ai).
- [WordPress.org Plugins](https://wordpress.org/plugins/) - Official WordPress plugin directory. Free submission, manually reviewed against the plugin guidelines. **Cost:** Free. [Submit here](https://wordpress.org/plugins/developers/add/).

## Review and comparison sites

Where buyers compare software. Claim a free profile early; reviews collect slowly, so start before you need them.

- [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net) - Crowdsourced software alternatives site. Add your product free with an account; it shows up as an alternative to competing tools. **Cost:** Free. [Submit here](https://alternativeto.net/software/new/).
- [Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice](https://www.capterra.com) - Gartner Digital Markets review network. One free vendor sign-up lists your product across Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice. **Cost:** Free. [Submit here](https://www.capterra.com/vendors/sign-up/).
- [G2](https://www.g2.com) - The biggest B2B software review site. Create a free product profile, then collect reviews. Paid seller tools are optional. **Cost:** Free (paid seller tools optional). [Submit here](https://sell.g2.com/create-a-profile).
- [SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net) - Business software directory with strong domain authority. Free listing; paid demand-generation programs drive buyer traffic to your page. **Cost:** Free + paid demand-gen. [Submit here](https://sourceforge.net/software/vendors/).
- [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com) - Consumer review platform. Claim a free company profile and collect reviews; paid plans add marketing and AI-visibility features. **Cost:** Free + paid plans. [Submit here](https://business.trustpilot.com).

## Newsletters and media

Big AI newsletters. These are paid promotions, priced per send. Best for a launch spike, not a first step.

- [Ben's Bites](https://bensbites.com) - AI newsletter with 170k+ subscribers. Product promotion runs through their self-serve ad store. **Cost:** Paid. [Submit here](https://ads.bensbites.com).
- [Superhuman AI](https://www.superhuman.ai) - AI newsletter read in 3 minutes a day. Paid ad sponsorships through their self-serve advertising page. **Cost:** Paid. [Submit here](https://advertise.superhuman.ai).
- [The Rundown AI (ads)](https://www.therundown.ai) - Daily AI newsletter reaching 1M+ readers. Paid sponsorships and takeovers; a separate free tool submission form exists (see Directories). **Cost:** Paid. [Submit here](https://www.therundown.ai/advertise-with-us).

## Communities

Free, but the currency is reputation. Read each community's rules, post from a real account, and share the story, not just the link.

- [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com) - Founder community for profitable online businesses. Share your product, milestones and revenue numbers. **Cost:** Free. [Submit here](https://www.indiehackers.com).
- [r/artificial](https://www.reddit.com/r/artificial/) - The main AI subreddit. Strict on self-promotion: share real research, demos and technical substance, not ads. **Cost:** Free. [Submit here](https://www.reddit.com/r/artificial/).
- [r/EntrepreneurRideAlong](https://www.reddit.com/r/EntrepreneurRideAlong/) - Founder subreddit for sharing what you are building and how. Story posts do better than bare links. **Cost:** Free. [Submit here](https://www.reddit.com/r/EntrepreneurRideAlong/).
- [r/InternetIsBeautiful](https://www.reddit.com/r/InternetIsBeautiful/) - Large subreddit for interesting websites. Heavily moderated: no basic or unoriginal sites, so only post something genuinely novel. **Cost:** Free. [Submit here](https://www.reddit.com/r/InternetIsBeautiful/).
- [r/SideProject](https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/) - Subreddit for sharing side projects and getting constructive feedback. Post from a real account and read the rules first. **Cost:** Free. [Submit here](https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/).

## How to use this list

- **Launch week:** pick one big stage (Product Hunt or a smaller launch platform if you want a real shot at the top 3) plus Hacker News if your product is genuinely ready to try. Prepare the post the day before.
- **Weeks 2-4:** submit to the free directories (Toolify, Future Tools, Dang.ai with a backlink, Toolpilot, SaaSHub). Paid directory listings are worth it only on sites whose audience matches your product.
- **Claim profiles early:** G2, Capterra, Trustpilot and AlternativeTo are free and take time to collect reviews. Start now, not when a buyer asks.
- **If your product fits a store:** a Chrome extension, WordPress plugin, GPT, Hugging Face Space or MCP server gets discovered inside the platform itself. That is usually better traffic than any directory.
- **Communities are not billboards:** accounts that only post their own product get banned. Comment, help, then share.
- **Paid newsletters:** only after you know your conversion numbers. A $1,000 send that converts at zero is an expensive lesson.
- **Be careful with paid listings:** prices change and some directories sell mostly backlinks. Check the current price on the site itself before paying.
